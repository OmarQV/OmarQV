# Agentes IA + Blockchain · El despertar de los agentes

An accessible introduction to AI agents, blockchain and Stellar for students and builders in La Paz.

## My role

I co-organized and hosted the event as part of the Crypto Coders core team. The public event page lists me among its hosts.

Crypto Coders and ADC organized the gathering, with support from the Blockchain Acceleration Foundation (BAF) and Stellar.

## Event details

- **Date:** Thursday, 29 October 2026
- **Time:** 16:00–18:00 (Bolivia time)
- **Venue:** sixth-floor auditorium, Computer Science program, Universidad Mayor de San Andrés (UMSA), La Paz, Bolivia

## Purpose

The session introduced students to how AI agents use tools, how they can interact with blockchain, and what permissions people should retain when an agent prepares a transaction.

## Programme

Four short talks moved from agent fundamentals to a Stellar transaction.
